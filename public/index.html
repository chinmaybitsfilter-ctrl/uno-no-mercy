UNO Show 'Em No Mercy rules

  • Goal: empty your hand first, or be the last player left.
  • Match the top card by colour, number or symbol. Wilds go on anything.
  • Draw until you can play: no match? Keep drawing until you get one — then you must play it.
  • Stacking: answer a draw card (+2, +4, +6, +10) with one of equal or higher value. The total passes on until someone can't stack — they take it all and lose their turn.
  • Mercy Rule: 25 or more cards in your hand and you're knocked out.
  • 7: swap hands with a player you choose. 0: everyone passes their hand to the next player.
  • Skip Everyone: you go again. Discard All: also dump every card of that colour.
  • Reverse: with 2 players it acts as a skip.
  • Wild Reverse +4: reverses, then the next player draws 4 (with 2 players, it bounces back to you).
  • Wild Color Roulette: the next player picks a colour and flips cards until that colour shows (wilds don't count). They keep them all and lose their turn.
  • UNO: tap UNO! before or as you go down to one card. If someone taps Catch first, you draw 2.
  • Scoring: winner gets numbers at face value, coloured actions 20, wilds 50, plus 250 per knockout.
